VAN DER WAALS DIMENSIONAL HOMOGENEITY CHECKER
( P + a / V² ) · ( V - b ) = R · T
1. Pressure Term Homogeneity:

[P] = [a / V²] ==> [a] = [P] · [V²]
= [M L⁻¹ T⁻²] · [L³]² = [M L⁵ T⁻²]

2. Volume Term Homogeneity:

[b] = [V] = [L³]

3. Transcendental Wave Function Rule:

In y = A · sin(ωt - kx):
• [ωt] = [1] ==> [ω] = [1/t] = [T⁻¹]
• [kx] = [1] ==> [k] = [1/x] = [L⁻¹]
• [A] = [y] = [L]

⚠️ NEET Homogeneity Limitation

Necessary but NOT Sufficient:
A dimensionally consistent equation is not necessarily physically correct!

Example: s = ut + at² is dimensionally homogeneous, but physically false because the factor 1/2 is missing!

Dimensional analysis can never verify pure numerical constants like 1/2, 2π, or √3.

NCERT SIGNIFICANT FIGURES & ARITHMETIC ROUNDING ENGINE
Total Significant Figures: 3 SF
• Leading zeros (0.00) are placeholders and NEVER significant.
• Digits 4 and 7 are non-zero (significant).
• Trailing zero (0) after decimal is significant because it reflects precision.
Addition / Subtraction Rule:

Governed strictly by LEAST DECIMAL PLACES.
436.32 (2 dec) + 227.2 (1 dec) = 663.52 ==> 663.5

Multiplication / Division Rule:

Governed strictly by LEAST SIGNIFICANT FIGURES.
4.237 g (4 SF) / 2.51 cm³ (3 SF) = 1.688... ==> 1.69 g/cm³
