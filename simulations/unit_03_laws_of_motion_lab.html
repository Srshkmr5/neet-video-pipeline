NEET TRAP: NORMAL FORCE DOES NOT ALWAYS EQUAL mg

When a pulling force is inclined upward at angle θ, the vertical component F·sinθ relieves ground pressure! Thus Normal reaction is N = mg - F·sinθ, strictly less than weight mg!

NEET TRAP: STATIC FRICTION IS SELF-ADJUSTING

Static friction is NOT fixed at μ_s·N! It precisely matches the applied force up to the threshold. If F_app = 10 N and f_s,max = 50 N, friction is strictly 10 N, NOT 50 N!
