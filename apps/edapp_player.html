NEET Trap: Dimensionality of Radian and Steradian

Having units does NOT imply having physical dimensions! Radian and Steradian have recognized SI units, but are strictly dimensionless [M⁰ L⁰ T⁰].

Has Unit ⇒ Must Have Dimension [M L T] ➔ rad & sr: Unit [rad, sr] but [M⁰ L⁰ T⁰] Dimensionless
